# Routing evidence disposition

The new sparse checkpoints have genuinely completed 5,005,312 tokens of synthetic training. Sampled route loads, soft entropy and CV over 61 logged steps are measured in phase1a_diagnostics.json and the raw experiment metrics. Early imbalance recovers; all experts receive tokens in the late sampled batches. None of this proves task/language specialization or useful physical locality.

Part E's substantially larger representative real-code/general/technical/multilingual traces, document-separated fit/held-out atlas, transition matrices, task/language histograms and equal-byte 1/2/4/8-expert packing comparisons were not collected. The explicit slowdown review stop was applied before that phase. Existing results/routes.jsonl and cache_simulation.json remain historical smoke evidence; they were not overwritten or relabelled as results from the newly trained checkpoints.

Specialization, natural locality, graph-packing benefits and task-profile cache initialization from these trained checkpoints are **unknown**. Existing simulator results are not physical offload or measured decode-throughput evidence. No locality-aware training was added.
